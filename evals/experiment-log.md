**Lesson (24 Nov):** Opus 5.5 was chosen for translation, descriptions and answers by default, without trying cheaper models first. Haiku 5.5 ($0.10 / $0.50 per million tokens, 40 times cheaper than Opus) would very likely have done the translation for about $0.02 instead of $0.75. Rule from now on: start with the cheapest model that could do the job, test it on a small sample, and step up only with evidence.

**Total so far:** $9.24 of the $10 budget.
